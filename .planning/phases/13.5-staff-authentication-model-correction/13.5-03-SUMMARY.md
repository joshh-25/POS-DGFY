## Decisions Made

- Real bcrypt detection is intentionally syntactic: `/^\$2[abxy]\$\d{2}\$/`.
- Reset findings name the reinvite path through `staff_invitations` but never quote legacy credential values.
- Credential related targets are written only after parent staff writes return `inserted` or `reconciled`; skipped/conflict parents do not fan out.
