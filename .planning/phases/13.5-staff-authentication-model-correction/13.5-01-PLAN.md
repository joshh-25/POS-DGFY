Missing or pending DGFY membership does not invalidate a tenant-local staff account (D-13.5-05).

    2. **Superseded-rules ledger** — a numbered list quoting or precisely naming each original sentence-level rule with its disposition (Pitfall 7 in 13.5-RESEARCH.md). Minimum entries, using the verified rule locations from read_first:
       - "IMS user invitations are DGFY-account invitations" and "tenant-local password setup for invited business users are retired by default" → SUPERSEDED: invitations create tenant-local staff with tenant-local credential setup; DGFY linking is a separate optional step.
       - "new invited users must be selected registered DGFY accounts" and the grace-cohort/`LEGACY_TENANT_LOGIN_GRACE_END=2027-06-17` post-deadline blocking of unlinked users → SUPERSEDED/REFRAMED: unlinked tenant-local staff are a permanent supported state, not a dated grace cohort.
       - POS DGFY-unlock and IMS switcher framing of tenant-local login as a dated fallback (lines 47-48) → REFRAMED: DGFY unlock/switching remain features of LINKED staff; tenant-local login is the baseline, not a fallback.

       - Behavior flags `LEGACY_TENANT_LOGIN_GRACE_ENABLED`, `LEGACY_TENANT_LOGIN_GRACE_END`, `DGFY_LEGACY_TENANT_REGISTRATION_ENABLED`, `DGFY_TENANT_USER_EMAIL_REPAIR_ENABLED` → document disposition: no key renames; the grace/deadline language no longer implies future removal of tenant-local staff login.
