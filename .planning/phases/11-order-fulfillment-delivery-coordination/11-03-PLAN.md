Wire the phase together at the two finalize seams and the composition root. Inject a `recordStageEvents` port (from Plan 02's `buildFulfillmentModule`) into the availments module so that (a) the POS/dine-in `finalizePersist()` transaction auto-writes the full 5-row stage-event sequence and sets the denormalized columns to `completed` (D-05), and (b) the online `finalizeStorefrontOrder()` transaction persists the previously-dropped `fulfillmentMode` and writes one `placed` stage event (D-06, closes Landmine 2). Output: `availmentRepository.js`, `availments/index.js`, `availmentUseCases.js`, `storefrontFinalizeUseCases.js`, and `routes/index.js` modified.

Landmine L2 (persist the dropped fulfillmentMode). Composition-root PORT wiring (recordStageEvents injected like recordSaleEffect/commitReservation). Discretionary resolutions surfaced: A1 (POS/dine-in defaults fulfillment_mode='dine_in' at finalize, with an optional controller override), A2 (online Availments enter at 'placed'; staff progress to confirmed — no auto-confirm on payment).
