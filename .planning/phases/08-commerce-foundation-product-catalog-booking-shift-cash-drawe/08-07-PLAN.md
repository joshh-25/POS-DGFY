<objective>
Build the booking module: create and cancel a Booking against a bookable service Product, blocking at branch-level concurrent capacity via an atomic guarded UPDATE that cannot oversell under concurrency, releasing the slot atomically on cancel, with dual-authorization cancel (staff/owner or the booking's own consumer account) and a reserved availment_id link for fulfillment.

Purpose: Booking is the second consumer of the Product catalog and the first place the atomic-guard concurrency pattern is exercised in apps/dgfy-api. The capacity race is a real correctness/security risk (overselling slots), so the guard must be proven under concurrent load.

Output: modules/booking with buildBookingModule DI factory. Depends on products (bookable Product + productRepository read) and inventory (reserved effect contract).
</objective>

    - When slots_remaining is 0 for that (product, branch, slot), the guarded UPDATE affects 0 rows and createBooking returns conflictError (409) with NO booking row written.

    - Concurrency: N simultaneous createBooking against a capacity-1 slot yields exactly 1 success and N-1 conflictError (no oversell).
