<objective>
Build the shifts module: open a shift with a declared cash float under the DB-enforced one-open-shift-per-cashier+terminal invariant, close it with an Expected-vs-Actual reconciliation whose formula anticipates Phase 9 inputs, and log every cash-drawer event (including a no-sale pop) to an append-only ledger. Stale shifts are flagged, never auto-closed.

Purpose: Shifts are the accountability precondition for Checkout (Phase 9 rejects a sale without an open shift). The one-open-shift invariant and cash-drawer audit trail must be race-proof and tamper-evident from day one.

Output: modules/shifts with buildShiftsModule DI factory. Note: the compliance gate is NOT wired into shift-open this phase (Phase 9 wires all gate call sites); the module is built to accept an injected assertComplianceGate later without restructuring.
</objective>
