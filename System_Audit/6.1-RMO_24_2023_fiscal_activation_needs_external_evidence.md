Severity: High
Audit area: Regulatory readiness, fiscalization

## Re-Baseline Update (2026-08-05)

**Still open — inherently an external-evidence gap; nothing in the repo can close it alone.** `docs/compliance/submission/filing-instructions.md` §5 still reads `Engineering approver: pending`, `Compliance approver: pending`, `Final filing batch ID: pending`. `docs/compliance/rmo-24-2023-gap-closure-plan.md` still states the system is not yet ready to claim full RMO 24-2023 compliance or BIR accreditation approval. `docs/compliance/evidence/` contains only encryption-verification and restore-drill artifacts — no accreditation, PTU/ATG, or eSales acknowledgement evidence anywhere. **Compounding issue found during re-baseline:** `docs/compliance/compliance-ops-regulatory-verification.md` commits to *monthly* regulator-source review but is itself last reviewed 2026-04-07 — roughly four months overdue on its own cadence. Track that lapse alongside this finding; it is the reason external evidence has not been chased down.

## Finding

Repository controls and documents can support fiscal-readiness work, but external BIR accreditation, filing, or signoff evidence remains outside what code-only checks can prove.

## Evidence

- `npm run check:compliance` passed.
- `docs/architecture/adr/0042-bir-rmo-24-2023-fiscal-document-and-accreditation-closure.md` governs the fiscal compliance closure path.
- The audit did not find current external accreditation or filing proof inside release evidence.

## Why This Matters

Internal compliance controls are not the same as legal or regulator acceptance. A system can pass internal checks while still needing external registration, accreditation, certification, or tax authority evidence before claims of full regulatory readiness.

## Required Remediation

1. Maintain the internal compliance controls and tests.
2. Track external BIR evidence separately from code compliance.
3. Link accreditation or filing artifacts to release readiness when available.
4. Avoid claiming full RMO 24-2023 production compliance until external evidence exists.

## Acceptance Gates

- Internal compliance check remains green.
- External fiscal evidence is attached or explicitly marked pending.
- User-facing or sales-facing claims separate internal readiness from official approval.
