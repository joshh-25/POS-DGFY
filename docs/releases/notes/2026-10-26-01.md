## Included

- POS terminals keep the store's last-known workflow when store settings fail to load, so an F&B store keeps Dine In and the Table # field instead of falling back to Walk-in/Pickup/Delivery, and settings reload automatically when the connection returns, without restarting the app. (#2824)

## Operational notes

This candidate has no staging soak — released as a main hotfix outside the normal promotion flow. See `docs/ops/RELEASE_CANDIDATE_POLICY.md`'s "Hotfix and back-port" section and the operator's route decision on #2824.

The fix is web-only: no API, migration, or schema change. A terminal stores its store-settings copy after the first successful settings load on this build, so a terminal that fails its very first load after the update still shows the Walk-in/Pickup/Delivery workflow until it reconnects; a reconnect prompt for that case is a separate follow-up.

Sales already recorded as `walk_in` for an F&B store before this release are not corrected by this release; correcting them is a separate, explicitly authorized production data change.
