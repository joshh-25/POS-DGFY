- Business registration has a redesigned desktop and mobile flow with location selection, business hours, menu and storefront image uploads, and draft recovery across navigation and refresh. (#2443; PR #2442)

- DGLaundry staging deployment automation and host-routing templates are included; the workflow still depends on separate host and GitHub environment setup. (#2387; PRs #2444, #2448)
