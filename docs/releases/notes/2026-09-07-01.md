## Included

  bulk endpoints. (#1643, epic #265)
- POS Items search, category/stock filters, totals, and pagination now query the complete
  authorized catalog instead of only the first preloaded page. (#1677)
- POS checkout now accepts an exact centavo-precision payment across Cash, GCash, Maya, Card, Bank
  Transfer, and QR Ph instead of rejecting a payment short by a fraction of a centavo due to
  rounding. (#1677)
