## Included

- Fixed a production error ("Data truncated for column 'reference_type' at row 1") that could
  block a cashier from re-parking a POS sale after resuming it and adding an item. The
  `stock_movements.reference_type` enum was missing the `POS_PARKED_SALE` value the parked-sale
  inventory writer uses. (#2321)
