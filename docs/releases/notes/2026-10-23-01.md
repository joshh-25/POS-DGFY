## Operational notes

This candidate has no staging soak — released as a main hotfix outside the normal promotion flow. See `docs/ops/RELEASE_CANDIDATE_POLICY.md`'s “Hotfix and back-port” section and the operator's route decision on #2715.

This candidate includes an additive landlord migration for `external_redirect_sites.show_on_map`; deploy the migration runner before the API.

Real partner HTTPS templates must be configured and enabled in Admin before temporary event pins can be applied. The seed creates only listings with real coordinates and an approved public display subset; it does not provide live sync or a DGFY-native booking, job application, taxi dispatch, or payment flow. Proventus pins represent employers, not job postings; no Eytitu taxi data is included. Clear temporary event pins after the program.
